About Us

Making green energy simple, reliable and profitable.

We are working for over the period of thirteen years to make human life easy, better, healthful and graceful — through Quality Power Backup Solutions, Green Energy Solutions (Solar Energy), and Water Processing Units.

Based in Bhandara, Maharashtra, we serve industrial, commercial and domestic customers. Every project is handled end-to-end: site assessment, design, procurement, installation, MSEB liaising and after-sales service.
