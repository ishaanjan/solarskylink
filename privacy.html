Information Collection and Use

Solar Skylink requires certain permissions to provide its core features, including:

  • Displaying rooftop solar products, brochures, and news.
  • Calculating solar position (sunrise, sunset, azimuth, elevation) based on your location.
  • Generating proposal / quotation PDFs and saving them locally on your device.
  • Fetching customer reviews and installation photos over the internet.
  • Sending you helpful reminders and tips.
  • Opening your dialer, WhatsApp, email, or maps when you tap a contact action.

Location Permission

The Solar Position Tracker feature uses your device's approximate or precise location to calculate accurate sunrise, sunset, solar noon, azimuth and elevation values for your rooftop. Location is only accessed when you actively tap the "Use GPS" button. Coordinates are used locally by the calculation engine and are not transmitted to our servers.

Recently computed locations may be stored locally in the app's database so you can revisit them from the History section. You can clear this data at any time via Settings → Clear Cache.

Storage Permission

The application generates PDF proposal documents and saves them in the app's private files directory. On Android 9 and older, the app may request storage permission to write these PDFs. On newer versions, PDFs are saved to the app-private folder and shared via Android's FileProvider — no broad storage access is required.

Generated PDFs contain only the data you entered on the Access Code screen (Customer Name, Kilowatt, Amount, Location, Date). They stay on your device unless you explicitly share them.

Notification Permission

The app schedules occasional notifications (daily solar tips, service reminders) using Android WorkManager. You can disable these at any time from Settings → Notifications. On Android 13 and above, you will be asked to grant notification permission the first time.

Phone Permission

When you tap "Call" from the Customer Care or Contact screens, the app opens your device's dialer with the pre-filled Solar Skylink phone number. The app itself does not place calls without your explicit action and does not access your call log or contacts.

Internet Access

The app connects to the internet to:

  • Fetch the latest customer reviews (from https://ishaanjan.github.io/solarskylink/reviews.json).
  • Load banner and customer installation photos.
  • Load Google AdMob advertisements.
  • Open external links (website, WhatsApp, email, maps).

If the network is unavailable, the app falls back to bundled offline content so most features continue to work without internet.

Data Storage

The application stores the following locally on your device:

  • Your app preferences (dark mode, language, notifications on/off).
  • Recently generated PDF proposals.
  • Solar position history entries.
  • Favorite locations for the Solar Tracker.
  • Cached customer reviews and content.

This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes all this data.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you and improve their own services:

  • Google Play Services

This app uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier (AAID) and non-personal usage data to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads from your device's Settings → Google → Ads. You can also disable ads in the app from Settings → Show Ads.

In case of an error in the app, we may collect diagnostic data via third-party services. This may include:

  • Device IP address (via ad SDKs)
  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Android operating system version
  • App version and configuration

  • Anonymised crash stack traces

Cookies

This application does not directly use cookies. However, third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob and Google Play Services) may use similar technologies to improve functionality and personalization.
